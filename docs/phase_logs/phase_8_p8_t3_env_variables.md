## New taxonomy class: code-default
  33 Settings fields carry safe in-code defaults (e.g. DEFAULT_PAGE_SIZE=20,
  MAX_PAGE_SIZE=100, APP_VERSION="0.1.0", OCR_MODEL="local"). Forcing these
  into hardcoded-safe or unused-staging would be inaccurate — they are read
  by the app but do not need staging overrides. Documented as code-default.
